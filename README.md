# 🎮 Pong Game

A classic Pong game built with HTML5, CSS3, and JavaScript. Play against an intelligent AI opponent in this nostalgic arcade experience with a modern neon aesthetic.

## 🎯 Features

- **Player vs Computer Gameplay** - Challenge the AI opponent
- **Smooth Controls** - Control your paddle with mouse or keyboard (Arrow Keys / W-S)
- **Intelligent AI** - Computer opponent adapts to ball movement
- **Physics Engine** - Realistic ball physics with spin mechanics
- **Collision Detection** - Accurate detection for paddles and walls
- **Scoreboard** - Real-time score tracking (First to 10 wins)
- **Neon Theme** - Modern dark theme with glowing neon aesthetics
- **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop and mobile browsers
- **Game Reset** - Easy reset button to start new games

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Open the Game**
   - Simply open `index.html` in your web browser
   - Or double-click the file to launch automatically

2. **Controls**
   - **Mouse**: Move vertically to control your left paddle
   - **Keyboard**: Use ⬆️ Arrow Up / ⬇️ Arrow Down or W/S keys
   - The computer automatically controls the right paddle

3. **Objective**
   - Score points by getting the ball past the opponent's paddle
   - First player to reach 10 points wins!
   - Click "Reset Game" to play again

## 📋 Game Rules

- Ball bounces off top and bottom walls
- Ball bounces off paddles with spin effect
- Score when opponent misses the ball
- Paddle collision applies spin to ball trajectory
- Game ends when a player reaches 10 points

## 🎮 Tips to Win

- Position your paddle at the center for better response
- Aim for the edges of the paddle to add spin
- Move your paddle early to intercept the ball
- Watch for ball patterns and predict movements
